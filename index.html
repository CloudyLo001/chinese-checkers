    How to Play

    Move all ten of your marbles into the corner directly opposite the one you started in. First player to fill it wins.

    Your turn

    Move one marble, in one of two ways:

    • Step into an empty hole next door, in any of the six directions.
    • Hop over a marble next door — yours or anyone's — into the empty hole directly beyond it. You may keep hopping as far as the board allows, and you may stop after any hop.

    You cannot mix a step and a hop in the same turn, and nothing is ever captured — hopped marbles stay exactly where they are.

    Super mode

    In Super Chinese Checkers a hop is not limited to the marble next door. You may jump a marble any distance away, as long as every hole between you and it is empty, and you land the same distance beyond it in the same straight line — with those holes empty too. The jumped marble ends up at the exact midpoint of your move.

    The ordinary hop is simply the shortest version of this, so everything else is unchanged: still one marble per turn, still chainable, still nothing captured. Marbles cross the board far faster, and games are much shorter. Pick the mode when you set up the game.

    Controls

    • Tap or click a marble to see its legal moves, then tap a ring to move there.
    • Your turn only passes on when you press End turn — no move ends it by itself. After a hop you can tap another ring to keep hopping first.
    • Tap the marble to put it back a leg at a time, until the turn is open again.
    • On a touch screen: two fingers to turn and tilt the board, pinch to zoom, double tap to reset the view.
    • With a mouse: right-drag (or shift-drag) to turn and tilt, scroll to zoom, double click to reset the view.

    Corners

    With 3 players every destination starts empty. With 2, 4 and 6 players your destination is another player's starting corner — you move in as they move out.
